Sarah: Got it. So if layered math systems and tuning dials have been around since the eighties, what did the transformer actually change?

Sarah: So it had terrible short-term memory.
Laura: Exactly. Then researchers introduced the transformer, and its big trick is a mechanism called attention. Attention lets the model look at every piece of the input text at the same time and dynamically score which earlier pieces are most relevant to what it's predicting right now.
Sarah: So it can reach back across the whole sentence and grab the important word?
Laura: Right. Think of troubleshooting a defect on a manufacturing line. If a sensor trips at packaging, you don't just check the station right before it—you look across the whole upstream process at once to see which step correlates with the fault.
